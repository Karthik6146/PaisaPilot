Privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026

Who runs PaisaPilot

PaisaPilot is a private, invite-only budget tracker. It is run by the site owner who gave you your invite code. Questions about your data go to them.

What we store

  • Your profile: the username and profile name you choose, and your password as a salted scrypt hash. The password itself is never stored.
  • Your records: everything you enter, such as transactions, budgets, sub-budgets, autopays, priorities, notes and loans. Loans can include another person's name and phone number if you add them.
  • Your sessions: a hash of each sign-in token, a request-verification token and an expiry time. Sessions last 7 days or until you sign out.
  • Sign-in limits: short-lived counters keyed by a keyed hash of your IP address and username, used to slow down password guessing. They expire after 15 minutes.

What we do not do

  • No ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No connection to your bank. Every number comes from what you type.
  • Fonts and scripts are served from PaisaPilot itself, so loading a page does not contact other companies.

Cookies and device storage

PaisaPilot sets one cookie, the session cookie that keeps you signed in. It is HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict, so scripts and other sites cannot read it. Your records are not kept in browser storage.

Where your data lives

The app runs on Vercel and stores data in a Supabase Postgres database. Both are infrastructure providers that host the data on our behalf. Your hosting provider may keep standard request logs, such as IP addresses, for a limited time.

When your data leaves the app

Only when you choose to. Sending a loan reminder opens WhatsApp with the message filled in, and exporting a backup hands the file to your device or share sheet. What happens after that is governed by those apps.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings & backup lets you download all your records as a JSON file at any time.
  • Correct or clear: edit any record, or clear all financial records while keeping your profile.
  • Delete: Delete profile removes your profile, records and sessions from the database straight away. Copies inside the hosting provider's own backups expire on its backup schedule.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with scrypt, sessions are stored only as hashes, and every change needs a verification token. No system is perfectly secure, so use a password you do not use anywhere else. Password recovery is not available, so keep yours safe.

Children

PaisaPilot is built for pocket money. If you are under 18, use it with a parent or guardian's permission.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes too. Significant changes will be shared with the people who hold an invite.
