# 🤝 Udhaar & Debt Tracker Guide

College friendships often involve shared expenses—canteen lunches, auto splits, emergency mobile recharges, and textbook xerox copies. The **Udhaar Hub** keeps these finances clear, stress-free, and respectful.

---

## 1. Core Distinction

| Type | Meaning | Card Color | Badge |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Money Lent** | You paid for someone; they owe you money | 🟢 Emerald | `Lent (Owes you)` |
| **Money Borrowed** | A friend paid for you; you owe them money | 🔴 Rose | `Borrowed (You owe)` |

---

## 2. Duration & Due Date Countdown Badges

Every record includes an active countdown based on the target return date:

- 🟢 `Due in X days`: Ample time remaining before the expected date.
- 🟡 `Due in 1-2 days`: Nudge state—payment window approaching.
- 🟠 `Due Today!`: Target settlement day reached.
- 🔴 `Overdue by Xd!`: Overdue badge automatically triggers a high-priority warning in the **Alerts Center**.

---

## 3. Partial Repayment Logging

Students often return money in partial installments (e.g. ₹150 today, ₹200 next week).

### How to Log Repayments:
1. Click **"Log Repayment"** on any active card.
2. Enter the partial amount received (or paid).
3. Add a payment note (e.g., *PhonePe transfer*, *Cash at canteen*).
4. The system updates:
   - The visual repayment progress bar (e.g., `Repaid: ₹200 of ₹450 (44%)`).
   - The timestamped payment audit history.
   - Automatically settles the debt when the total matches the original amount!

---

## 4. 1-Click WhatsApp Friendly Reminders

Asking friends for money back can feel awkward. The **WhatsApp Reminder Generator** crafts polite, culturally relatable messages:

### Templates Generated:
- **For Money Lent:**
  > *"Hey Priya! Hope you're doing great. Just a friendly reminder about the ₹250 from our earlier split for 'Project printouts'. You can UPI/GPay me whenever you get time. Thanks!"*
- **For Money Borrowed:**
  > *"Hey Aman! Just wanted to let you know I'm preparing to return the ₹250 I borrowed for 'Canteen lunch'. Please send your UPI ID or QR!"*

If a phone number was provided, it automatically opens `https://wa.me/[phone]?text=...`. Otherwise, it copies the polite message straight to your clipboard.

---

## 5. Victory Celebrations

When a debt is fully cleared (via either complete repayment or clicking **"Settle in Full"**):
- A burst of **canvas-confetti fireworks** animates across the screen.
- A triumphant 4-note victory chime plays via Web Audio API.
- The debt transitions to the **Settled** archive tab.
